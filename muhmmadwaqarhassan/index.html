Muhammad Waqar Hassan is a visionary entrepreneur and the driving force behind CabXperts, a professional taxi dispatching service transforming how transportation businesses operate across the United Kingdom.

With a deep understanding of the transportation industry and a passion for technology-driven solutions, Muhammad has built CabXperts from the ground up — creating a company that empowers taxi operators with reliable dispatching, efficient operations, and modern customer support.

His leadership philosophy centers on combining industry expertise with innovative technology to deliver seamless mobility experiences. He believes in building lasting relationships with clients, fostering growth for businesses, and continuously pushing the boundaries of what is possible in transportation.
